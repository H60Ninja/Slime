Source: `~/Desktop/Squish! Slime for Kids - Feedback Report - squish_feedback.pdf` (their template report).

## Listing description refresh (paste into Play Console with v1.0.2)

Full:

    Squish! is a calm sensory slime toy for toddlers and kids. Pick a colour, pour it in, and mold real slime with your fingers: press craters, pull strands, pinch ridges, roll it flat, slice it with the knife, and stamp stars, hearts, moons and fish into it. Mix colours like paint and watch new ones appear.

    Ten kinds of slime, each with its own squishy sounds: shiny, clear, metallic, cloud, crunchy, butter, fluffy, glow, holographic and colour-changing magic slime. Add beads, stars and sprinkles. Save your favourite slimes in jars and take a picture for the gallery.

    Made by a dad for his own kids:
    • No ads, no purchases, no internet
    • Nothing to read, so toddlers can play on their own
    • Nothing collected, nothing leaves the tablet
    • Works in portrait and landscape on phones and tablets

    Satisfying, fidget-friendly sensory play for ages 2 and up. Squish, stretch, mix, repeat.
