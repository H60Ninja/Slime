# Slime — Privacy Policy

**Effective 2026-10-17**

Slime is a toy for young children. It is designed to be used without an internet
connection and it collects nothing.

- **No data collection.** Slime does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
  information, device identifiers, usage data, or analytics.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request the network permission and makes no
  network connections.
- **No advertising.** There are no ads and no ad SDKs.
- **No accounts, no purchases.** Nothing to sign in to and nothing to buy.
- **No third-party services.** No analytics, crash reporting, or social features.
- **Local settings only.** The chosen slime colour and sparkle setting are saved on the
  device so they are remembered next time. They never leave the device and are deleted
  when the app is uninstalled.
- **Saved slimes.** The jar shelf keeps up to six slimes inside the app's own private
  storage on the device. They are removed when the app is uninstalled.
- **Snapshots.** The camera button saves a picture of the slime into the tablet's own
  Pictures folder (a "Slime" album) so it can be shown to a grown-up. The picture stays
  on the device. Slime never reads other photos.
- **Permissions.** The only permission used is vibration, for a tiny haptic tick when
  the slime is poked. Saving a snapshot uses the standard Android media store and needs
  no storage permission.

This policy applies to all versions of Slime on the Amazon Appstore and Google Play.
