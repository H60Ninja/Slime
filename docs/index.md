# Squish! Slime for Kids

A calm slime toy for little hands. No ads, no purchases, no internet, nothing to read.
Press craters, pull strands, pinch ridges, mix colours like paint, stamp, slice, and keep
your favourites in jars.

- Available on the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets. Google Play coming soon.
- [Privacy policy](privacy.html) (short version: the app collects nothing).
- Made by DadBuilt Apps. Source on [GitHub](https://github.com/H60Ninja/Slime).
