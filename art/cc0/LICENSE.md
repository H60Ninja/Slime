# CC0 recordings used as the body layer of the game's sounds

All files in this folder are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; it is
given here anyway. `art/make_sfx.py` decodes them with ffmpeg and mixes them under the
synthesized layers; nothing in this folder ships as-is.

| Files | Pack | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| squish_01..06, squishpop, squishsplat_impact (.mp3) | Squish Sounds Effects | EZduzziteh | https://opengameart.org/content/squish-sounds-effects |
| mud_07, mud_08, mud_12, mud_13, mud_24 (.ogg) | 25 CC0 mud sfx | rubberduck | https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-mud-sfx |
| slime_01..16, splash_06, splash_10, bubble_03, loop_bubbles_1 (.ogg) | 40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX | rubberduck | https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx |

Also evaluated, not used: "8 wet squish, slurp impacts" (Independent.nu via qubodup, CC0):
too long and splattery for a kids' toy.
